Keyboard tests

The test sends two Tab presses in a fresh page, requires the button to have focus, then presses Enter or Space and expects a count of 1. Two tests pass for C; two fail for A and two for B, in each of the two datasets. Reports preserve all eight failures and four passes. The A/B test processes exit with code 1.

keyboard.spec.cjs · playwright.config.cjs

A failed test can be an expected experimental finding. It remains a failure of the required behaviour.

Lighthouse scores

The contrast warning concerns NOTES, drawn on the notebook. Lighthouse reports 4.36:1 on the original. After changing only the colour, all three scores become 100/100, while the keyboard barriers on A/B remain observed. agent-accessibility-tree passes in all six reports; this is not a pass for the entire Agentic Browsing category.

Why 96 for B and 95 for C? The contrast failure has weight 7 out of 129 applicable points for A/C. B adds four passing ARIA audits with combined weight 28: (157 − 7) / 157 ≈ 95.54%, versus (129 − 7) / 129 ≈ 94.57%. The displayed score is rounded.

Control focusability (focusable-controls) is a manual check, excluded from the automated score.

Limitations
